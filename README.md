## The word

> SMARTYPANTS

Grandma has met the machine that knows every answer — every recipe,
every birthday, every fact she ever forgot — and she has decided what
it is: a smartypants. She says it the way she says it to a grandchild
who corrects her at the dinner table: half scolding, half proud,
completely baffled that a little box knows more than she does after
eighty years. It is her warmest insult, and she means it with love.

## How it works

`main.py` is a deterministic program that writes the single word above
to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly eleven bytes, no trailing newline, no
randomness, no network. Run it locally (the grader sandbox writes to
`/output`):

```sh
python3 main.py
```
